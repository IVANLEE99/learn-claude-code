Common Non-compliant Channels Content Overview
常见的不合规频道内容概述
Non-compliant content in Channels generally includes the following types:
在频道中，那些不符合规范的内容通常包括以下类型：

1. Violations of account  1. 账户违规行为

Including but not limited to:
包括但不限于：

1.1 Frequently posting non-compliant activity;
1.1 频繁发布不符合要求的活动内容；

1.2 Frequently using non-compliant information;
1.2 经常使用不符合要求的信息；

1.3 Sending non-compliant messages to harass other users;
1.3 发送不符合要求的消息来骚扰其他用户；

1.4 Frequently posting risky content without qualifications to do so;
1.4 经常在不具备相关资格的情况下发布危险内容；

1.5 Posting a large number of marketing, spamming, or vulgar comments;
1.5 发布大量营销类评论、垃圾信息或粗俗的言论；

1.6 Maliciously providing contact information (e.g. the provided Weixin account is suspected of spreading information about pornography, gambling, or drug, or selling prohibited items.)
1.6 恶意提供联系信息（例如，所提供的微信账号被怀疑用于传播与色情、赌博或毒品相关的内容，或者用于销售违禁物品。）

2. Infringing content  2. 侵权内容

Including but not limited to the content that:
包括但不限于以下内容：

2.1 Exploits the identity information of others;
2.1 利用他人的身份信息；

2.2 Infringes upon the legal rights and interests of others such as the right of name, right of portraiture, and right of reputation;
2.2 侵犯他人的合法权益，例如姓名权、肖像权以及名誉权；

2.2.1 Maliciously disclosing the privacy of others, such as ID information, contact information, etc.;
2.2.1 恶意披露他人的隐私信息，如身份识别信息、联系信息等；

2.2.2 Maliciously damaging the reputation of celebrities;
2.2.2 恶意损害名人的声誉；

2.2.3 Insulting or slandering others;
2.2.3 侮辱或诽谤他人；

2.2.4 Intimidating, tracking, and secretly taking photos of others to pose a threat to others' personal safety;
2.2.4 通过恐吓、跟踪以及秘密拍摄他人的照片来威胁他人的人身安全；

2.3 Infringes upon the copyrights and trademark rights of others by, for example, plagiarizing the content containing the copyright marks of others;
2.3 通过抄袭他人享有版权的内容等方式，侵犯他人的版权和商标权；

 2.3.1 Plagiarizing others' works without permission;
2.3.1 未经许可抄袭他人的作品；

2.3.2 Posting content that is highly similar to others' works and suspected of plagiarism without permission.
2.3.2 发布与他人作品高度相似的内容，且涉嫌抄袭的行为，同时未获得许可。

3. Vulgar content  3. 普通内容

Including but not limited to the content that:
包括但不限于以下内容：

3.1 Displays persons in revealing dress or is suspected of being naked;
3.1 穿着暴露的服装出现的人；或者涉嫌裸体的人；

3.2 Involves sexual suggestions through text, sounds, items, gestures, or behavior;
3.2 涉及通过文字、声音、物品、手势或行为来传达性暗示；

3.3 Contains insulting and humiliating words;
3.3 包含侮辱性和贬低性的言辞；

3.3.1 Abusive, vulgar, lewd, or other malicious comments;
3.3.1 包含辱骂、粗俗、色情或其他恶意内容的评论；

3.3.2 Discriminatory content that is offensive to others' dignity;
3.3.2 具有侮辱性、会损害他人尊严的内容；

3.4 Goes against social ethics;
3.4 违背了社会道德规范；

3.4.1 Promoting wrong values of marriage and love, such as extramarital affair;
3.4.1 宣扬错误的婚姻与爱情观念，例如提倡婚外情行为；

 3.4.2 Presenting outdated vulgar customs, such as wedding horseplay and child brides;
3.4.2 宣扬过时的、低俗的习俗，比如婚礼上的嬉戏行为以及童婚现象；

3.5 Displays improper behaviors of minors or is harmful to the physical and mental health of minors;
3.5 展示未成年人不当行为的内容，或者对这些未成年人的身心健康造成危害；

 3.5.1 Displaying sensitive body parts of minors;
3.5.1 展示未成年人的敏感身体部位；

3.5.2 Displaying minors smoking or drinking;
3.5.2 展示未成年人吸烟或饮酒的场景；

3.5.3 Displaying or suggesting intimacy between minors;
3.5.3 展示或暗示未成年人之间的亲密关系；

3.6 Displays the details of animal mating;
3.6 显示动物交配的详细信息；

3.7 Focuses on the private parts for a long period.
3.7 长时间专注于私处部位。

4. Contains aggressive marketing or harassing content
4. 包含攻击性营销手段或骚扰性内容

Including but not limited to the content that:
包括但不限于以下内容：

4.1 Involves pyramid schemes;
4.1 与金字塔式传销体系有关；

4.2 Solicits donations without the qualification for public fundraising;
4.2 未经适当授权，就进行募捐活动；

4.3 Involves trading or dissemination of non-compliant software and services (such as plug-ins);
4.3 涉及交易或传播不符合要求的软件和服务（如插件）；

4.4 Promotes medicines, medical devices, health foods, and formula foods for special medical purposes;
4.4 推广用于特殊医疗目的的药品、医疗器械、健康食品以及配方食品；

4.5 Uses plagiarized content to increase traffic and promote;
4.5 使用抄袭的内容来增加网站流量并促进宣传；

4.6 Uses plagiarized content to increase ad traffic;
4.6 使用抄袭的内容来增加广告流量；

4.7 Completely consists of marketing content without any valuable information;
4.7 完全由营销内容组成，没有任何有价值的信息；

4.8 Promotes risky products and sensitive business without qualifications;
4.8 无资质地推广高风险产品和敏感业务；

4.9 Uses inflammatory content to recommend or sell products or services;
4.9 使用煽动性的内容来推荐或销售产品或服务；

4.10 Focuses on medical treatment, health care, regimen, and life content to attract attention and promote products by highlighting their efficacy.
4.10 专注于医疗治疗、医疗保健、养生之道以及生活内容等方面，通过突出产品的功效来吸引注意力并推广产品。

4.10.1 Aims to promote or sell medical products/services, such as medicine, devices, consulting services or courses, etc.;
4.10.1 目标是推广或销售医疗产品/服务，例如药品、设备、咨询服务或课程等；

4.10.2 Aims to promote or sell products/services related to medical cosmetology;
4.10.2 旨在推广或销售与医疗美容相关的产品或服务；

4.10.3 Publishes medical information and increases traffic by providing contact information;
4.10.3 发布医疗信息，并通过提供联系方式来增加流量；

4.10.4 Contains medical marketing information violating the "Advertising Law";
4.10.4 包含违反《广告法》的医疗营销信息；

4.10.5 Promotes products/services by claiming efficacy expressly or implicitly.
4.10.5 通过明确或隐含地宣称产品/服务的有效性来推广它们。

5. Illegal, non-compliant, or disturbing content
5. 非法、不符合规定或令人不悦的内容

Including but not limited to the content that:
包括但不限于以下内容：

5.1 Promotes pyramid selling activities and related products;
5.1 促进金字塔式销售活动及相关产品的推广；

5.2 Promotes gambling activities;
5.2 促进赌博活动；

5.2.1 Organizing profit-making gambling by use of video games;
5.2.1 通过电子游戏来组织盈利性赌博活动；

5.2.2 Promoting gambling-related products, platforms, or plug-ins;
5.2.2 推广与赌博相关的产品、平台或插件；

5.2.3 Promoting short-term high-yield investment products;
5.2.3 推广短期高收益投资产品；

5.2.4 Promoting entertainment activities involving money in the name of playing board games;
5.2.4 推广以玩棋盘游戏为名的、涉及金钱的娱乐活动；

5.2.5 Promoting game room card or related agents without qualifications;
5.2.5 未经许可就推广游戏厅卡片或相关代理服务；

5.3 Involves violence or terror, or is bloody or scary;
5.3 涉及暴力或恐怖行为，或者情节血腥可怕；

5.3.1 Maliciously tarnishing the public image of public officials;
5.3.1 恶意诋毁公职人员的社会形象；

5.3.2 Promoting or displaying illegal violence;
5.3.2 宣扬或展示非法暴力行为；

5.3.3 Disturbing content such as close-ups of surgery, or images making trypophobia sufferers uncomfortable;
5.3.3 应避免出现令人不适的内容，比如手术过程的特写镜头，或者那些会让恐群症患者感到不适的图像。

5.3.4 Promoting or displaying cruelty to animals;
5.3.4 宣扬或展示对动物的残忍行为；

5.4 Involves items controlled or prohibited by the People's Republic of China (PRC);
5.4 涉及由中国人民共和国控制或禁止的物品；

5.5 Sells lottery tickets without permission or promotes illegal lottery games;
5.5 未经许可出售彩票，或推广非法的彩票游戏；

5.6 Publishes financial information such as fund-raising, online lending, and cash-out without permission;
5.6 未经许可就公开财务信息，如筹款情况、在线贷款数据以及资金变现情况；

5.7 Incites ethnic hatred and ethnic discrimination, or undermines ethnic unity;
5.7 煽动民族仇恨和歧视，或者破坏民族团结；

5.7.1 Violating ethnic customs and publishing extreme nationalism information;
5.7.1 违反民族习俗，发布极端民族主义信息；

5.7.2 Promoting racial and ethnic discrimination, and causing antagonism in terms of regional discrimination, etc.
5.7.2 促进种族和民族歧视，引发地区间的对立等。

5.8 Opposes PRC's religious policies, or promotes cults and feudal superstitions;
5.8 反对中国政府的宗教政策，或者宣扬邪教和封建迷信思想；

5.9 Spreads false information, disturbs public order, and undermines social stability;
5.9 传播虚假信息、扰乱公共秩序、破坏社会稳定；

5.10 Promotes immoral values or wrongly-oriented concepts;
5.10 宣扬不道德的价值观念或错误导向的思想；

5.10.1 Deliberately showing overeating;
5.10.1 故意表现出暴饮暴食的行为；

5.10.2 Displaying details of banned movies and books;
5.10.2 显示被禁止的电影和书籍的详细信息；

5.10.3 Promoting and displaying wealth-flaunting behaviors of minors, campus bullying, etc.;
5.10.3 宣扬和展示那些令人反感的行为，比如未成年人炫耀财富的行为、校园欺凌等现象；

5.10.4 Promoting anti-humanism, taking pleasure in natural calamities or others' misfortunes, etc.
5.10.4 宣扬反人道主义思想，以自然灾难或他人不幸等为乐等。

5.10.5 Promoting and abetting the concept of PUA (pick-up artist), selling PUA courses, or sharing PUA techniques, etc.
5.10.5 推广和教唆“搭讪艺术”的概念，出售与搭讪艺术相关的课程，或者分享搭讪技巧等。

6. Inducing content  6. 诱导内容

Including but not limited to the content that:
包括但不限于以下内容：

6.1 Induces users to share, like, comment, or follow under the guise of interactions or playing games;
6.1 以互动或游戏为幌子，诱导用户进行分享、点赞、评论或关注；

6.2 Coerces users into sharing, liking, commenting or following by issuing malicious curses or threats;
6.2 通过发出恶意诅咒或威胁，迫使用户分享、点赞、评论或关注某人或某个内容；

6.3 Incentivizes users to share, like, comment or follow using physical objects, virtual objects, and false information.
6.3 激励用户通过实物、虚拟物品以及虚假信息来分享、点赞、评论或关注他人。

7. Lewd content  7. 色情内容

Including but not limited to the content that:
包括但不限于以下内容：

7.1 Involves prostitution and sexual services;
7.1 涉及卖淫和性服务；

7.2 Disseminates and sells lewd information resources;
7.2 传播和销售色情信息资源；

7.3 Involves sexual organs, sexual behaviors, and sexual details.
7.3 涉及性器官、性行为以及与性相关的内容。

8. Misuse of feature  8. 功能的滥用

Including but not limited to the content that:
包括但不限于以下内容：

8.1 Is suspected of using fake numbers to promote;
8.1 涉嫌使用虚假号码进行推广活动；

8.2 Contains extended links irrelevant to the video content to increase traffic;
8.2 包含了一些与视频内容无关的扩展链接，目的是增加流量；

8.3 Mentions (@) a large number of users or includes unrelated topics in activity descriptions in the attempt to increase traffic;
8.3 在活动描述中提到大量用户，或者包含与主题无关的内容，只是为了增加流量；

8.4 Is published partially to incentivize users to tap extension links for more content or next episodes.
8.4 这部分内容的发布是为了激励用户点击扩展链接以获取更多内容或下一期剧集。

9. False content  9. 虚假内容

Including but not limited to:
包括但不限于：

9.1 Politics-related false content that violates laws and regulations, endangers the security, or harms the interests of the People's Republic of China;
9.1 涉及政治内容的虚假内容，如果这些内容违反了法律法规，或者威胁了国家安全，或者损害了中华人民共和国的利益，那么这类内容也是被禁止的。

9.2 Social event-related false content that disrupts social order, or causes or may cause a public panic;
9.2 与社交活动相关的虚假内容，这类内容会扰乱社会秩序，或者引发或可能引发公众恐慌；

9.3 False content about public topics, food safety, and healthcare;
9.3 关于公共话题、食品安全和医疗保健方面的虚假信息；

9.4 Other content that has been officially announced to be untrue.
9.4 其他那些已被正式宣布为不真实的内容。

10. Repeated posting of risky content without qualifications
10. 不断发布具有风险的内容，且未作出任何说明或警告

Including but not limited to:
包括但不限于：

10.1 The content related to medical treatment or healthcare that is posted without qualifications such as that of doctor, nurse, pharmacist, medical institution, or pharmaceutical company;
10.1 那些未经授权而发布的、涉及医疗治疗或健康保健内容，例如来自医生、护士、药剂师、医疗机构或制药公司的信息；

10.2 The content related to finance, credit loans, wealth management, investment, stocks and other financial fields that is posted without the qualifications to engage in such industries as banking, security, futures, insurance, and funds.
10.2 所有涉及金融、信贷贷款、财富管理、投资、股票等金融领域的内容，如果发布者不具备从事相关行业所需的资格，比如银行、证券、期货、保险或基金行业的相关资格，那么这些内容就不被允许发布。

11. Cheating behaviors  11. 作弊行为

Including but not limited to:
包括但不限于：

11.1 Committing cheats through non-compliant means, such as using third-party platforms, plug-ins, or system vulnerabilities, in Weixin or related services;
11.1 通过非合规手段进行作弊行为，例如利用第三方平台、插件，或者利用微信及相关服务中的系统漏洞来进行作弊；

11.1.1 Falsifying data such as the number of followers, views, comments, and likes;
11.1.1 伪造数据，例如伪造粉丝数量、观看次数、评论数和点赞数等；

11.1.2 Performing unusual operations such as trading in accounts and registering false accounts;
11.1.2 执行一些不寻常的操作，比如交易账户、注册虚假账户等；

11.2 Displaying or promoting methods or tools for cheating.
11.2 展示或推广用于作弊的方法或工具。
